Field Guide

WordStrata Excavation Corp

The Mission

Dig deep. The surface is just dirt, but the Bedrock (Dark Tiles) holds the fortune. Reach Depth 50 to discover the Progenitor... then descend into the infinite abyss beyond.

Physics & Geology

● Gravity: Things fall. Use it to crash tiles together.
● Explosives: Bomb tiles clear a 3x3 crater.
● Multipliers: Darker strata layers grant 2x, 3x Points.

Advanced Excavation

● Strata Collapse (8+ Letters): Long words trigger collapse—cleared tiles instantly refill!
● Blind Streak: Submit without checking validity. Build streaks for Multipliers.
● Theme Words: Mining vocabulary earns special bonuses.

Tools of the Trade

● Supply Drops: Call in fresh tiles when stuck.
   Normal Mode: Earn them by leveling up.
   Survival Mode: Rare! You can only earn them by leveling up.

● Jokers (?): Wildcards. Tap to morph them into any vowel.

● Undo: Reverse your last move. Limited in Survival Mode.

Scanner Settings

Configure your heads-up display in the Pause Menu.

HINTS: ON Green light for good words. Best for rookies.
HINTS: OFF Scanner offline. +20% Score Bonus.

Game Modes

● Normal: Practice mode. Take your time, earn high scores.
● Survival: Race against oxygen depletion. Limited resources.
● Challenge: Compete with others using shared seed codes.

⚠️ Survival Protocol

● O2 Depletion: Air runs out constantly. Submit words to breathe.
● Scarcity: Undos and Drops are limited. Spend them wisely.
● No Refreshing: Close the tab and your run ends. No mercy.
